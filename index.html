FAQ

Common Questions

What is a custom prospect database?

It is a targeted list of businesses built around your selected industry, location, fields, and filters.

How accurate is the data?

We clean and organize the data before delivery, but business information changes over time, so outreach teams should still review critical records.

Can you provide emails?

Yes, emails can be included where available or prioritized as part of the research scope.

Can you target specific cities?

Yes. You can request one city, multiple cities, full states, multiple states, or nationwide coverage.

How is FlowingProspects different from a generic lead list?

The database is built to your specifications instead of pulled from a one-size-fits-all directory.

Can I request a custom industry?

Yes. Choose Custom Industry or Other in the request form and describe the businesses you want.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery can be requested for 24 hours, 48 hours, 3 days, or 1 week depending on scope.
